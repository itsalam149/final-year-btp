Complete Frontend & System Overhaul (v1.1.0):
  • 1. Multi-Tab Navigation Hub: Full 4-tab bottom navigation: [Assistant], [Notes & Hisaab], [Reminders], and [Audit & Stats].
  • 2. Visual Notes & Hisaab Explorer: Dedicated visual ledger screen with live search, currency totals, formatted breakdowns, and item deletion directly backed by encrypted Room SQLite storage.
  • 3. Visual Reminders & Alarms: Dedicated alarms screen with due times, priority badges, and one-tap completion linked to Android AlarmManager.
  • 4. Live Security Audit & Hardware Telemetry: Live throughput meter, battery energy cost (Joules/token), thermal status, and cryptographic audit log of all autonomous tool invocations.
  • 5. Quick Action Chips Carousel: Horizontally scrollable one-tap action chips for compound arithmetic hisaabs, bill-splits, YouTube playback, and alarms.
  • 6. Dual Reasoning Core: Program-Aided Language (PAL) symbolic arithmetic + GBNF grammar-constrained decoding over ARM NEON SIMD.
